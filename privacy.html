Privacy Policy: Hushwood uploader

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What this app is

Hushwood uploader is a private tool used only by Hushwood Production Studios. It uploads our own finished short videos to our own YouTube channel on a schedule. It is not available to the public and has no other users.

What Google data it uses

The app asks for one permission only: upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It cannot read, change or delete other videos, comments, playlists, subscriptions, or any other Google account data.

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on our own computer, in a private file readable by our account alone. It is never sent anywhere except to Google to upload our videos. We do not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data or Google user data to anyone.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which deletes the app's permission immediately.
